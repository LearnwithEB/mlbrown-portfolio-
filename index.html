01 — About ML

Research & Scholarship

ML Brown is a doctoral candidate in Special Education at the Penn State College of Education, where his research centers on the intersection of race, disability, and educational equity. As a non-traditional and neurodivergent scholar, he brings lived experience to his academic work, examining how teacher perceptions and implicit bias shape the educational trajectories of Black students labeled with Emotional and Behavioral Disorders (EBD). He also serves as an Adjunct Professor at Delaware State University.

[PHOTO: ML Brown presenting research or teaching]

IRB-Approved Dissertation Research

The Miseducation of Black Boys with EBD: Black Parental Fears of Teacher Perceptions and Implicit Bias

This qualitative study examines how Black parents of boys labeled with EBD perceive and navigate teacher bias within special education systems.
