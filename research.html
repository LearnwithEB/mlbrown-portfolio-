Research & Scholarship

Examining how race, disability, and educational systems intersect — and what that means for the students and families those systems too often fail.

IRB-Approved Dissertation Research

“The Miseducation of Black Boys with EBD: Black Parental Fears of Teacher Perceptions and Implicit Bias”

This qualitative study examines how Black parents of boys labeled with Emotional and Behavioral Disorders perceive and navigate teacher bias within special education systems. Penn State University, Principal Investigator: ML Brown.
